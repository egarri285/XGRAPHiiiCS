05 — THE STUDIO

About us

A print shop where design and production live under one roof.

We turn artwork into physical space — printed, finished and installed with obsessive attention to color and craft.

XGRAPHiiiCS is a small & large format print studio specializing in canvas fine art, custom wallpaper and murals. We handle both the design and the production for hotels, restaurants and residential projects, plus textiles, stickers, banners and specialty effects.

From eco-solvent printing for stretch materials to UV inks for chrome and iridescent finishes, our shop runs professional-grade equipment to deliver consistent, durable, gallery-quality results — at any scale.
